This is a script that was written for a soliloquy during my undergrad (which failed to get into production for several reasons). Enjoy the read :)

## FLASHBACK

*(A mother is talking to her unborn baby (named Blake) who is currently in her womb. The father is sitting just across the mother)*

**Mother**
The world is gonna lie… Blake. Will squirrel you from the dark and tell you tales of demons and ghouls. Never in our existence will there be a comforting gloom to have an ear for. Never… But you do. Although your memories fade, it will keep echoing within the bounds of my womb until the next arrival. The spritzing rain and the bubbling water; The bantering oblivion and the chattering heartbeat. Sounds heard by me and you. One day, you and I will be holding hands under the drizzle. You are just weeks away from bidding my comforting gloom, a goodbye. And that day isn’t far.

**Father**
What did our baby learn today?

**Mother**
We are living in a house that is the size of a coffin and you were sitting right across. Are you deaf by choice or muted by your ever-running thoughts and worries?

**Father**
Not this again! We have had a million versions of this discussion in a million ways, I have said things that I shouldn't have!

**Father**
……Yes, I was aggressive, but then again, you haven’t stepped outside this room for thirty weeks! Here….grab a newspaper and I’m sure you’ll see them searching for us, waiting for the both of us to return. Us,and just us. How do you think we could possibly explain Blake to them?

*(father looks around the room and finds the word ‘Umbra’ written all over)*

**Father**
Umbra? What does that even mean? ughh….nevermind that, but what if these thoughts you’re having affect our baby? Imagine if our fetus could hear… No. I do not think we are ready for this. The only way to put off all this uncertainty is to not have the baby. Tell our parents a convincing reason and attribute that to our missing. It will be a second chance at life.

**Mother**
You are shooting questions that were shot a year ago. Trust me. You are not gonna regret it. He will be the symbol of love. He will be our second chance! When he kicks…

**Father**
No, don’t,don’t… don’t look at me like that. Don’t. Don’t.Don’t… Don’t you dare talk to me like that. If his kicks are more important, here… enjoy mine!

**BLAKE SCREAMS**

## BLAKE’s MONOLOGUE

*(You wake up, look around the room and find yourself tied to a chair. Opposite to you is a wall. It bears a white circular patch of light, and the shadow of a baby whose navel seems to be connected to the circumference of the circle via what seems to be a shadow of the placenta. That whole patch of light acts like a womb for the baby in the shadow. There is a table right in front of you. A well-grown Blake is getting up from the floor in front of you. It is possible that you have been abducted by him, but you do not know why!)*

And someone said the fall hurts all of one’s senses. For the one with none… I lost all of it when I opened my eyes to a brighter world. I lost all of it when life wasn’t just a game, but a blame game… with each finger pointing to the other. And I wasn’t warned about becoming the victim. At 3 days old, I attended my mom’s funeral. Died in childbirth. There came the fingers, raging towards me at an age where I couldn’t understand directions. Each of them had a thing to say. “Had the cursed child not been born; She would have been alive”. “The child must have died instead”. What should I grieve about? My mother, who I couldn’t see, who I couldn’t touch… Is it that I will never get to hear her? Or is it that she doomed me to the life of an outcast, forever carrying a curse in an abandoned room?

*(Sound of bottle rolling towards Blake dominates the room. Blake stops the rolling bottle with his foot, takes it up and places it on the table. He is fascinated by the beer bottle)*

Now … If humans have six senses and you had four cans of beer while driving, how many senses do you have? … I take this silence of yours as a None. And what did it cost? A huge pool of blood in front of your car. And you were adamant as to not drop that last can of beer when I took you from it.

Why do people drink? To end their personal misery?

Now… Now I get it. When one’s marionette isn’t proper, you can’t expect the beast they are riding on, with its two orby eyes four feet apart, to be under control. It’s gonna hunt for food that must never have been. *(Blake whispers to you)* Of the owner’s kind. So, it pounces on a hungry pedestrian who is resting under his blankets trying to make ends meet. Funny I found you on one such beast named ‘Audi’. Even under the rumbling of its engine, you were oblivious of putting an end to a poor soul’s suffering . Why do people drink? To end misery that might not necessarily be theirs!

*(The baby falls down on the table from the womb in the shadow along with the placenta. It falls in front of you. The shadow was casted by a toy that looks like a baby, with a knife in its navel playing the role of placenta. Blake reacts, picking it up and examining it as he speaks)*

Darkness hides an identity and the light… it just shows another. That mellifluous voice never fails to impress me with beautiful anecdotes like these. Too soft, too soothing to be human. It tells why ‘what’ happens, from behind the darkness. The darkness in that cellar-like room where the fingers abandoned me was never so comforting. The way it pronounces ‘Umbra’, it’s like a ‘she’ said- it promised me to extend its hand under the drizzle.

Now you see what those fingers were capable of.

*(Blake shows the doll to you and the knife on it, and places it on the table)*

Nobody deserves to be hated. But my father… who chose to remain the finger rather than a helping hand… is ruthless, arrogant… Whatever he is, even if he’s dead, I will wake him up to remind him that he is dead.

*(Blake gets up and starts pacing)*

If my memory serves right, inside your car were Tom, Danny and Elsa. They had nothing more to say, they were all empty beer bottles. I presume it was Jimmy in your hand when I took you. Loneliness… That thin line between the animate and the inanimate, is dwindling. Quite evident from the names you have given to the beer bottles.

*(Blake stops in front of you)*

You know the curse upon these beer bottles and innocent toys you and I have named? They have to hear. Hear all of one’s melancholy and oblige by the sheer aggressiveness of it. The irony is they are thrown when in need. Either when one’s anger is vented or in desperation.

*(Blake looks at the doll and speaks to it)*
You need not have listened to me. But it is what it is. I took him, cut the strings and tied him taut to my fingers. He swayed the way I wanted him to. Moved the way I wanted him to.. And look at him now! He can smile when I want him to. *(Blake laughs)* He can hear me and react to all of what I have to say.

*(Blake leaves the doll on the table)*

If all wants to live and none wants to die, then fear is the most innate. Fear… The language that the world will hear… and speak.

Had my mother lived, I wouldn’t have held my father at gunpoint two weeks ago. If he had let her live. Honestly, I never thought that guns were this light. More than the fear in his eyes, he was more under control. Under mine. The gun kept getting lighter and lighter as if it were a marionette.

Now, do you think I pulled the trigger and he died? He died. But I didn’t pull the trigger. Why would I? The world used me, reused and threw me as if I were a puppet. Now it’s my turn to give back. Finally get others to listen and react to what I have got to tell. That is the story of my first puppet – My own father. The fingers that pointed to me were now tied to my own fingers. Under my control now!

That’s when I realised - If the world’s a great puppet show, I want to be the best puppeteer.

You know the problem with my puppets? They are mortals. I am running out of them. My father literally ran away hours ago. Little did he know that he was running to his own grave. Then comes a car and hits him. I found him there, on the ground in a pool of blood. Inside the car, I found you. Everybody’s first is special. My father was special. And you … you are more special. Of course, it’s never that easy to fit in with me.

*(Blake hands a gun to you)*

You and me… we aren’t so different. We both killed in our obliviousness. You killed the form and I killed the soul before turning people to puppets. We are better than murderers. Think about it. Enough of running. Now is the time *(Blake taps the gun)* we make others run, the way we want them to.

You and me… Together, we can become the best puppeteers humanity has ever seen.

*(You raise the gun and aim it at Blake. You come back to full consciousness and Blake notices it)*

Oh! Look who’s back. Welcome. When I first held the gun, it was a matter of taking chances. There’s just a bullet in it. What are the chances? Either shoot me… End it all here… Or… Feel the marionette and use me.

Choose wisely. The…

*(You shoot Blake and he collapses)*

---

*(A spotlight is projected on the wall opposite to you, bearing the shadow of the master puppeteer behind her. It talks to you, but doesn’t reveal itself. You can only see the shadow on the wall)*

Darkness hides an identity, and the light… It just shows another! a beautiful puppet show, wasn't it?

*(You look down on Blake lying amidst a pool of blood, and he isn’t the one talking!)*
Hey, Don’t look down on my puppet you just shot. Who are you? You were living others’ definition of who you are.

*(The chair you are seated on is dragged to a dark room)*

So, did I steal a puppet from a much larger puppet show out there? Your masters will come in search of me. But can never find me. Because I don’t live in a defined space. I lurk beneath their shadows, in a place far away from one’s consciousness… which I call-

*(You are pushed into a dark room and the title rolls in)*

# **UMBRA**

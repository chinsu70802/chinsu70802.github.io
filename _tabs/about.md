I love reading elegant proofs in math! I love writing prose! 

This blog intends to convey my love for both, with a hope that it will inspire people to appreciate both forms of art, and possibly get some inducted into it. 

I learn better when I write about something. It invokes my spirit of curiosity, and helps me form questions. This blog is a way for me to learn a concept better, while giving back to the community that I learnt and benefitted from. 

Occasionally, I will be posting stories as well.
